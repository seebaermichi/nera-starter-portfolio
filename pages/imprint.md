## Information pursuant to § 5 DDG

What an imprint typically names (the exact duty depends on your country and
on what the site does):

- who runs the site: full name, or company name and legal form
- a postal address where you can be reached
- fast ways to contact you: email address, and often a phone number
- the person responsible for the content, if the law asks for one
- register entries and VAT ID, if you have them
