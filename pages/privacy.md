## What a privacy policy typically covers

The exact duty depends on your country and on what the site does:

- who is responsible for the data processing, and how to contact them
- what your hosting provider logs (such as IP addresses) and for how long
- every plugin, form, font, embed or analytics tool that processes visitor
  data, its purpose and legal basis
- how long data is kept
- the visitor's rights (access, correction, deletion, objection) and where to
  complain

## The contact form

As shipped, the contact form sends nothing over the network. When a visitor
submits it, a small script on this site opens the visitor's own email program
with the message filled in; the message then travels like any other email
they send, and you receive it in your mailbox. Name how you handle and keep
these messages.

## Sitemap

The site publishes a `sitemap.xml` for search engines. It lists the public
pages and contains no personal data.

## Cookies and device storage

As shipped, this site stores nothing on a visitor's device and reads nothing
from it, so no consent under § 25 TDDDG is needed for it. It loads no fonts,
scripts or images from other hosts. Check this again whenever you add a
script, an embed, a map or a plugin.
