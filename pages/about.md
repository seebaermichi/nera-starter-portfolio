## The studio

A one-person studio for graphic design and illustration. The work moves between personal series of abstract prints and commissions: posters, book covers and small visual identities.

## How a project runs

1. A short call about what you need, for whom and by when.
2. A first round of sketches, then one direction worked out in colour.
3. Final files for print and screen, with the colours documented.

## Tools

Pencil and paper first, then vector software; prints are made by a local risograph and giclée studio.
